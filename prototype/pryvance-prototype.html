What a job may do Read the ledger, group and rank what it finds, phrase an explanation, and name the options a person could choose between.
What a job may never do Write to the ledger, move money, change an allocation, alter a budget, or compute a total the application should have calculated itself.
Where the work happens A local model on your own machine, reached over the private network. Prompts and results never leave it, and every run is logged with its inputs.
Why alerts, not annotations Commentary sprinkled through the interface becomes noise you learn to ignore. One queue you can empty keeps the signal, and makes an empty queue mean something.
